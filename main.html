Calculation method & assumptions

Mole-balance calculation using ideal-gas inventory and normal-volume equivalence.

Initial nitrogen inventory (Nm³ equivalent):

N₂ inventory = V × (Pg + Patm) / Patm

Air introduced:

Air introduced = Q × t / 60

Final oxygen concentration:

O₂ (vol%) = 100 × [0.21 × Q × t / 60] ÷ [V × (Pg + Patm) / Patm + Q × t / 60]

Q = air flowrate (Nm³/hr); t = injection duration (minutes); V = system volume (m³); Pg = initial gauge pressure (bar(g)); Patm = 1.01325 bar(a). Air oxygen content is taken as 21 vol%.

  • Initial gas is assumed to be pure nitrogen (0% O₂).
  • System volume is rigid and remains constant.
  • Pressure is the initial pressure before air injection.
  • Temperature is assumed constant; ideal-gas behaviour applies.
  • Complete mixing is assumed, with no venting, leakage, purge outlet or gas withdrawal.
  • Injected air increases system pressure.
Process-safety limitation: Actual oxygen distribution can be non-uniform, especially near the injection point. Do not use calculated values alone to authorise vessel entry, hydrocarbon introduction, inerting or purge completion. Follow approved site procedures and verify with a calibrated analyser at representative sampling points.
